# 30/60/90 Formation Plan

The 30/60/90 plan moves the PPMO from design into governed operation.

## First 30 days — establish visibility and authority

Focus on confirming sponsor expectations, defining PPMO purpose and boundary, establishing decision-rights draft, building first initiative inventory baseline, defining taxonomy and minimum intake fields, and identifying governance forums and reporting pain.

Outputs: formation brief, operating charter draft, decision-rights draft, portfolio baseline v0, intake model draft, and governance cadence proposal.

## Days 31-60 — pilot governance

Focus on piloting intake with a small set of real requests, validating taxonomy against current initiatives, running the first governance council session, testing reporting views with executive sponsors, and capturing decisions, actions, risks, and unresolved ownership.

Outputs: intake pilot results, taxonomy adjustments, governance council agenda and decision log, reporting model v1, and evidence-gap cleanup plan.

## Days 61-90 — stabilize and hand off

Focus on locking formation assumptions requiring executive confirmation, moving active intake into the intake triage system, routing approved work into scoring or chartering as appropriate, using executive review cadence for portfolio decisions, and moving ongoing decisions/actions/risks into governance operations log.

Outputs: formation package v1, confirmed handoff rules, 90-day operating review, and next-quarter improvement backlog.

## Planning guardrail

Do not promise maturity in 90 days. The goal is governed operation, not a fully optimized PPMO.
