PPMO Formation Kit — Sample Output

This is a shortened illustrative output using the fictional NimbusWorks scenario. It demonstrates the shape of the formation package, not a final client deliverable.

Formation brief

ElementDraft
Formation triggerNimbusWorks has roughly 500 initiatives with inconsistent ownership, no trusted baseline, side-channel intake, and competing CIO/CFO/COO priorities.
Operating problemLeadership cannot make reliable tradeoffs because work is not consistently captured, classified, sponsored, or routed to the right decision forum.
PPMO purposeCreate enough structure to make portfolio work visible, comparable, sponsor-owned, decision-ready, and governed without creating a heavy process office.
Human confirmationsExecutive sponsor, portfolio scope, decision authority, mandatory vs discretionary work rules, and reporting data owners.

Operating charter excerpt

  • Owns: portfolio visibility, intake structure, taxonomy, governance cadence, decision preparation, reporting model, decision/action follow-through pattern.
  • Does not own: funding approval, risk acceptance, executive priority decisions, delivery ownership, headcount assignment, tool administration, or project execution.
  • Initial service model: intake facilitation, portfolio baseline cleanup, governance agenda preparation, decision-log maintenance pattern, and executive reporting support.

Handoff brief

  • To Portfolio Intake and Readiness Triage System: intake fields, intake states, routing outcomes, and taxonomy.
  • To Portfolio Prioritization Scoring Agent: confirmed taxonomy, funding class, strategic themes, and scoring boundary assumptions.
  • To Executive Portfolio Review Pack Builder: governance cadence, reporting views, decision-owner fields, and escalation rules.
  • To PMO Governance Operations Log: decision-log pattern, carry-forward fields, action owner fields, and unresolved assumptions.

Readiness view: Ready with caveats. Human confirmation is still required for sponsor authority, finance thresholds, council membership, and source-system ownership.
