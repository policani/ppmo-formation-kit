# Governance Council Design

A governance council should exist to make or prepare decisions, not to host status theater.

## Council design questions

- What decisions must this forum make?
- Which decisions are only prepared here and escalated elsewhere?
- Who must attend because they own authority?
- Who contributes evidence but does not decide?
- What inputs are required before a topic reaches the agenda?
- What outputs must be captured after the meeting?

## Forum pattern

| Forum | Purpose | Inputs | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intake review | Classify and route new demand | Intake record, sponsor, basic value/risk/readiness | Accept for discovery, defer, reject, reroute |
| Portfolio review | Review active portfolio health and tradeoffs | Portfolio baseline, risks, blockers, dependencies | Decisions, escalations, tradeoff asks |
| Executive council | Resolve priority, funding, capacity, and risk tradeoffs | Decision briefs, options, constraints | Approved decisions and sponsor actions |
| Operating closeout | Preserve follow-through | Decisions, actions, risks, due dates | Updated log and next agenda inputs |

## Council output

Produce governance forums, decision scope by forum, required attendees and roles, cadence, agenda pattern, required inputs, required outputs, escalation rules, and decision-log requirements.

## Guardrail

If a forum has no decisions, outputs, or escalation path, challenge whether it should exist.
