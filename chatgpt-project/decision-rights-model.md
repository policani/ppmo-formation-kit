# Decision-Rights Model

Decision rights must be explicit before intake, reporting, and governance cadence are treated as final.

## Decision categories

| Decision category | Typical owner | PPMO role |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic priority | Executive council | Provide tradeoff evidence |
| Funding approval | Finance and executive sponsors | Surface cost and constraint implications |
| Intake acceptance | Portfolio governance forum | Apply intake criteria and route work |
| Sequencing | Executive council or delegated portfolio forum | Provide dependency and capacity view |
| Scope change | Sponsor and delivery owner | Record impact and escalation path |
| Risk acceptance | Accountable executive or risk owner | Make risk visible; do not accept it |
| Exception approval | Named authority | Document exception and expiration |
| Pause/cancel | Executive sponsor or council | Prepare decision brief; do not decide |

## RACI pattern

For each decision, define accountable decision owner, recommender, required contributors, required evidence, forum where decision is made, escalation path, and decision-log field.

## Decision-rights output

Produce a table with decision type, decision owner, input evidence, forum, PPMO role, what is not the PPMO's role, escalation trigger, and human confirmation required.

## Guardrail

If a decision owner is unknown, mark it as unresolved. Do not assign final authority by inference.
