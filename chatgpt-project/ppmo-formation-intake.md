# PPMO Formation Intake

Use this file before drafting formation artifacts.

## Intake questions

Ask only what is needed. Group questions and limit the first pass to ten or fewer.

### Formation reason

- Why is the PPMO being established or reset now?
- What pain is leadership trying to remove?
- What failure pattern should not continue?

### Portfolio scope

- Which work is in scope: projects, programs, products, transformation, technology, finance, operations, regulatory, customer, AI, or all enterprise change?
- Which work is explicitly out of scope?
- Is the function advisory, controlling, reporting, facilitative, or decision-support?

### Executive sponsorship

- Who sponsors the PPMO?
- Which executives need a shared tradeoff view?
- Who can approve intake, funding, sequencing, escalation, and exception decisions?

### Current-state evidence

- Approximate initiative count.
- Known active work, stalled work, duplicate work, and unowned work.
- Existing meetings, tools, reports, templates, and decision logs.
- Known pain points from teams and executives.

### Data and reporting reality

- What data is trusted today?
- What data is missing, stale, inconsistent, or manually maintained?
- Which reports exist but are not decision-useful?

## Intake output

Produce formation trigger, current-state diagnosis, PPMO scope hypothesis, stakeholder and sponsor map, known facts, working assumptions, evidence gaps, human confirmations required, and recommended next artifact.

## Quality bar

The intake is complete enough when it can answer: "What operating problem are we solving, for whom, with what authority, across which work?"
