PPMO Formation Kit — Synthetic Sample Data

All data is fictional and public-safe. NimbusWorks is a made-up SaaS company used only to demonstrate the workflow.

Scenario

NimbusWorks has grown from one product line to four. Leadership estimates roughly 500 initiatives across product, engineering, finance systems, customer operations, data, security, and go-to-market operations. There is no trusted portfolio baseline, sponsor ownership is inconsistent, and the CIO, CFO, and COO are competing for shared engineering, data, and process-improvement capacity.

Current symptoms

  • Executives receive status decks but cannot see tradeoffs.
  • New work enters through chat, executive asks, roadmaps, incident follow-up, and finance planning.
  • Some work is mandatory, some strategic, some duplicate, and some stale.
  • No single definition exists for project, program, enhancement, platform work, operational fix, or transformation initiative.
  • Finance asks for benefits confidence; technology asks for capacity realism; operations asks for delivery dates.

Formation prompt context

The CEO has asked the CIO and CFO to propose a practical PPMO setup within 30 days. The goal is not a heavy PMO. The goal is enough structure to know what exists, who owns it, what decisions are needed, what work should stop entering through side channels, and what tradeoffs executives must make.
